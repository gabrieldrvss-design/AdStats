Your data in AdMobby

AdMobby is an independent Android dashboard for your AdMob statistics. This policy explains the information the app accesses, how it uses and stores it, and how you can remove it.

Effective and last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Information the app accesses

Live statistics require you to authorize a Google account with AdMob access. You can use the demo without connecting a Google account.

Google profile

Your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile photo URL identify the connected account and display its profile.

Publisher and app details

Your publisher ID, currency, reporting timezone, and app identifiers, names, platforms, and public store identifiers support account selection and app reporting.

AdMob reports

Estimated earnings, impressions, clicks, requests, matched requests, and available reporting breakdowns are used to show totals, comparisons, charts, rates, and CSV reports.

Local preferences

Appearance, language, and connection or demo preferences are stored on your device to remember your choices.

Breakdowns may include dates, countries, ad units, ad formats, platforms, mediation sources and groups, app/OS/SDK versions, and other supported reporting dimensions. These are AdMob reporting data; the app does not request individual ad viewers’ identities, contacts, messages, or precise device location.

2. Google authorization and use

AdMobby requests read-only AdMob access and Google identity, profile, and email permissions. It uses these permissions to retrieve and display your account and reports, refresh statistics, and create exports you request. The app cannot create or change your ads, mediation settings, or AdMob account through its read-only access.

Authorization is handled by Google. AdMobby does not receive your Google password. It holds access tokens in memory for API requests and does not save them to its report cache or preferences, or request offline refresh tokens. Google Play services manages its own authorization information under Google’s policies.

AdMobby’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used for the reporting features described here, not sold or used for advertising, credit decisions, or training generalized AI models.

The app does not send your Google profile or AdMob reports to a developer-operated server. The developer has no automatic access to your saved reports. If you voluntarily send information for support, it is used to address your request.

3. Storage and retention

The last complete live report is saved in the app’s private storage with the publisher details, Google profile, reporting context, last-update time, warnings, and app icon URLs. This lets the app show previous statistics while fetching updated data. It is replaced by a later successful report and has no fixed automatic expiry.

Disconnecting or switching to demo clears that saved report. Explicitly reconnecting or changing publisher also clears it before a new report is loaded. A failed refresh may leave the previous report available.

Downloaded profile pictures, app icons, and flags may remain in the image cache. Exported CSV files are kept temporarily in the app’s cache; files older than seven days are removed when another export is created. Disconnecting does not separately erase image caches or exported files. Android may clear cache files earlier.

Preferences remain until you clear app storage or uninstall. The app disables Android backup and excludes its data from configured cloud backup and device transfer. Copies you save or send outside AdMobby are controlled by you and the receiving service.

4. Third-party services and sharing

  • Google: Google Play services and Google API endpoints handle authorization, profile retrieval, and AdMob reports. Profile photos are downloaded from the photo URL Google supplies. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • App stores and image hosts: AdMobby may request public Google Play or Apple App Store pages using your linked app’s public store identifier to find its icon, then download that icon from the returned image host. See Apple’s privacy information for Apple services.
  • Country flags: Flag images are requested from FlagCDN / Flagpedia using the country code shown in the report.
  • Exports you choose: Android’s share sheet gives the app or destination you select access to the generated CSV. The file contains the reporting data for your selected report and filters. That recipient’s handling is governed by its own policies.

Online services receive the network information needed to respond, such as your IP address, requested URL, and HTTP request metadata. Google authorization tokens are not sent to the icon or flag services, and report contents are not uploaded to those image services. Providers may process requests on infrastructure in other countries under their own policies.

AdMobby does not include an advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. It does not sell your personal information or use your reports to target advertisements.

5. Security

API and image requests use HTTPS. Saved reports and preferences are held in Android’s app-private storage. The report cache does not contain access tokens. Protection of on-device data also depends on your device’s operating system, screen lock, and security configuration; no storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

When sharing a CSV, AdMobby grants the chosen destination access to that file through Android’s sharing mechanism. Share reports only with recipients you intend to give access to your reporting data.

6. Your controls and deleting data

  1. Disconnect: Use the disconnect control in Account to end the local session and remove the saved report and profile from the report cache. This does not revoke Google’s authorization grant.
  2. Revoke Google access: Open your Google Account’s third-party connections, select the connection used by AdMobby, and remove its access. Also clear local app data if you want previously saved information removed from your phone.
  3. Remove all app-held data: In Android settings, open Apps → AdMobby → Storage and clear storage, or uninstall AdMobby. This removes its private reports, preferences, image caches, and cached exports from the device.
  4. Remove external copies: Delete CSVs you saved elsewhere and contact recipients if you want shared copies deleted. AdMobby cannot erase files or records held by other apps or services.

AdMobby does not create a separate hosted user account. Disconnecting, clearing storage, or uninstalling does not delete your Google or AdMob account or the source reports held by Google.

7. Privacy inquiries

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information. Use the contact below for questions or requests about AdMobby. Source Google account information and AdMob records are managed through Google.

If you email the developer, your address and the information you choose to include are used to respond to your inquiry and retained as needed to handle it or meet applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords or access tokens. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete data stored only on your device; use the local controls above.

8. Policy changes

This policy will be updated when AdMobby’s data practices change, with a revised date on this page. If Google data will be used in a new way that requires consent, the app will disclose that change and request consent before that use begins.
